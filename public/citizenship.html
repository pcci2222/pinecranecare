Frequently asked questions

Are citizenship classes free?

Many are — several community organizations offer free N-400 and citizenship-interview prep in Chinese.

Do they help with the N-400 form and interview?

Yes — most cover form completion, civics test practice and interview preparation.

Does Kakatong verify organizations?

No — public info and community reviews only.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
