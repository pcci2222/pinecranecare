Frequently asked questions

Is competition math (Olympiad) offered?

Yes — several centers offer 奥数 / math-olympiad tracks alongside grade-level tutoring. Check each listing.

What ages?

Programs run from early elementary through high school; many families start young to build fundamentals.

Does Kakatong verify centers?

No — public info and community reviews only.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
