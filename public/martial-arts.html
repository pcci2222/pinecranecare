Frequently asked questions

What martial arts are offered?

Taekwondo is the most common, along with Kung Fu, Karate, Judo and mixed programs. Kakatong lists schools for each.

What ages?

Many schools start children around age 4–5; K-8 is the core range on Kakatong Kids.

Does Kakatong verify schools?

No. Public info and community reviews only.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
