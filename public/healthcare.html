Frequently asked questions

Is HHA training offered in Chinese?

Yes — several NY DOH-approved programs teach HHA/PCA training in Mandarin and Cantonese; some community organizations offer it free.

Can I work as a home aide after HHA training?

Yes — HHA certification lets you work with home-care agencies, and you can list yourself on Kakatong Care to reach NYC families directly.

Does Kakatong verify programs?

No. We list public information; confirm DOH approval and details with each program.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
