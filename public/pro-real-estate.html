Frequently asked questions

Do I need a state-approved course for a NY real estate license?

Yes — NY requires a 75–77 hour approved qualifying-education course before the licensing exam. Kakatong lists approved schools that teach it in Chinese.

Are courses taught in Mandarin or Cantonese?

Several listed schools teach in Mandarin and/or Cantonese; a few also offer Korean or other languages. Check each listing.

Does Kakatong verify schools?

No. We list public information; confirm NY DOS approval and details with each school directly.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
