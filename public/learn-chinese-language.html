Frequently asked questions

Are classes in Mandarin or Cantonese?

Most Chinese-language schools in NYC teach Mandarin; some community schools also offer Cantonese. Check each listing.

Are there weekend Chinese schools?

Yes — many programs run on weekends and after school, which is the traditional format for Chinese-language education.

Does Kakatong verify schools?

No. Listings show public information and community reviews.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
