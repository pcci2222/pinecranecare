Frequently asked questions

Do these schools teach in Chinese?

Yes — the schools listed here offer instruction in Mandarin, Cantonese or other Chinese languages, which most mainstream driving schools do not.

Do they help with the road test?

Most offer road-test preparation and, in many cases, use of a car for the test. Confirm with each school.

Does Kakatong verify schools?

No. Listings show public information and community reviews.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
