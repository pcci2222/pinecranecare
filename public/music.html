Frequently asked questions

What's the most popular instrument?

Piano, by a wide margin, followed by violin. Kakatong lists studios and private teachers for both.

What ages can start music lessons?

Many studios accept children as young as 4–5 for piano and violin; check each program.

Does Kakatong verify teachers?

No. Kakatong shows public info and community reviews; evaluate each teacher yourself.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
