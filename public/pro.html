Frequently asked questions

What adult training is listed?

Real estate & insurance licensing, HHA programs, Chinese-language driving schools, coding/tech bootcamps, citizenship (N-400) prep, and professional English (ESL).

Are there Chinese-language driving schools?

Yes — several NYC driving schools teach in Mandarin, Cantonese and other languages.

Does Kakatong verify schools?

No. We list public information and community reviews; confirm licensing and fit with each school.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
