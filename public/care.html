Frequently asked questions

Are the home aides Chinese-speaking?

Most listed aides and agencies serve the Chinese community in Cantonese, Mandarin and English. Search by ZIP or city and read reviews to find the right fit.

How much does home care cost in NYC?

Rates vary by care type and hours; many Chinese-community arrangements fall in the $20–$30/hour range. Confirm directly with each provider.

Does Kakatong verify providers?

No. Kakatong lists public information and community reviews. Interview candidates, check references and do your own due diligence before hiring.
