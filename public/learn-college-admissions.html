Frequently asked questions

When should we start college consulting?

Many families begin in 9th–10th grade to plan coursework and activities, though services span all high-school years.

Do consultants help with essays?

Yes — essay strategy and editing is a core part of most college-admissions services.

Does Kakatong verify consultants?

No — public info and community reviews only.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
