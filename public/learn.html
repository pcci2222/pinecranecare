Frequently asked questions

What test prep do Chinese families search most?

SHSAT (for Stuyvesant, Bronx Science), SAT and AP are the most common. Kakatong lists centers specializing in each.

Are there Chinese-language schools listed?

Yes — weekend and after-school Mandarin/Chinese programs are a dedicated subcategory.

Does Kakatong verify tutoring centers?

No. Listings show public information and community reviews; verify credentials and fit yourself.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
