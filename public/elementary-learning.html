Frequently asked questions

What's the difference from Kakatong Learn?

Kids Elementary Learning focuses on younger children's after-school enrichment; Kakatong Learn covers test prep and subject tutoring for older students.

Do programs offer homework help?

Yes — after-school homework support is a common feature.

Does Kakatong verify programs?

No — public info and community reviews only.

Kakatong 家家通 is a community directory and review platform for Chinese-community families. We list providers' public information and community reviews; we do not independently verify providers. Contact support@kakatong.app.
