Frequently asked questions

When should my child start SHSAT prep?

Many families begin in 6th or 7th grade to prepare for the 8th-grade SHSAT, though timelines vary by student. Ask each center about their program length.

What's the difference between SHSAT and SAT?

The SHSAT is the admissions test for NYC specialized high schools (taken in 8th grade); the SAT is a college-admissions test taken in high school.

Does Kakatong verify tutoring centers?

No. Kakatong shows public information and community reviews; evaluate each center yourself.
